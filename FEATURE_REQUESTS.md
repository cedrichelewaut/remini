# Remini review mining — feature request findings

Source: `reviews_output.json` (9,827 reviews, Play Store + App Store, 20 countries) +
manual multilingual reading. Generated by `extract_reviews.py` (English) and
`classify_multilingual.py` (14 non-English languages), plus hand-read samples
noted inline. Last updated against the dataset committed alongside this file.

## Coverage status

- **10,627 total reviews** fetched (9,510 App Store across ~19 countries capped
  at ~500/country by Apple's RSS feed; 1,117 Play Store, still rate-limited on
  most countries — see "Open issues" below). Grew from 9,827 as later CI runs
  pulled additional recent reviews, merged in rather than replacing prior data.
- **English: 3,888 reviews**, fully screened (regex classifier + manual read of
  everything that didn't match a category).
- **Non-English: 6,739 reviews (63% of corpus).** 4,783 covered by
  `classify_multilingual.py` across 14 languages (es, tr, pt, it, fr, vi, th, ja,
  ko, de, id, pl, nl, ru) — request-signal + category-keyword classification, plus
  a full manual read of every review that matched a signal but no category.
- **Not yet screened: ~1,956 reviews (18% of total)** — mostly "unknown"
  language (too short to classify, mostly emoji/one-word praise, low expected
  value) plus a long tail of 60+ languages at 1-80 reviews each (Malay,
  Arabic, Romanian, Ukrainian, Hindi, Chinese, etc.) — individually too small to
  justify a dedicated pattern set, but available if a specific market becomes a
  priority.
- **Verdict: we have NOT exhausted the review pool, but we've covered the large
  majority of it (82%) including every language with meaningful volume.** The
  main way to get more signal is (a) more countries (Apple's ~500/country cap is
  the binding constraint, we use 20 of ~175 storefronts), not re-reading what we
  have, and (b) fixing Play Store's throttling, which is still capping us well
  below its real ceiling.

## Priority segment: AI-generated photos of multiple people (couples focus)

This is the best-evidenced, most actionable finding in the dataset — corroborated
independently across English, German, Japanese, and Dutch reviews from unrelated
users in different markets.

**A couple/multi-person AI generation feature exists (or existed) and is
inconsistently available:**
- *(EN, Play/CA)* "They took away all the **couples' editions**"
- *(EN, App/AU)* "I wanted to try the **couple AI photo couple generator** but it
  was not listed"
- *(DE, App/DE, 5★)* "You can also generate yourself **as a couple/pair**
  ('zu zweit')... themed packages (50s style, photoshoot, kids, vacation)" —
  confirms it exists and works for some users
- *(JA, App/JP)* "The **kiss feature** that used to exist was really fun! I'd
  love for it to come back even temporarily" — a second, independent report of a
  removed couple/intimacy-oriented feature

**When it works, users want more of it:**
- *(EN, App/GB)* "Ability to **create more couple photos with individual photos
  of each person**"
- *(NL, App/NL, 5★)* "Especially with headscarf and **couple photos** there's
  little choice. Hopefully more get added." — also flags a representation gap
  for hijab-wearing users

**The core bug, corroborated across 3 markets:**
- *(EN, App/AU)* "It added 2 strangers to a group photo"
- *(EN, App/AU)* "Enhance a group shot photo but it does only one person
  clearly, blurry the others"
- *(DE, App/DE, 3★)* "With the **partner pictures**, one photo is always very
  inaccurate — simply a different person. Tried this with **several couples**."
- *(NL, App/NL, 1★)* "I input a photo of a **group of people** and **everyone
  got the same face**" — full identity collapse, more severe than "blurry"
- *(DE, App/DE, 1★)* Even solo AI Photo generation: "the generated photos don't
  even remotely resemble my face" — the identity-accuracy problem isn't unique
  to multi-person, but multi-person makes it worse/more visible

**Adjacent bias finding (2 independent reports):**
- *(EN, App/GB)* Won't generate accurate images of women with certain body
  types; over-corrects toward "flat chested"
- *(NL, App/NL, 1★)* "It doesn't account for skin color — dark skin tone
  suddenly becomes lighter after enhancement"
- *(EN, Play/CA)* "Despite being black I had white hands and brown ha[nds]..."

**Recommendation:** treat this as one underlying issue (the AI Profile/generation
system appears architected around a single identity, and breaks down — face
mixing, identity collapse, skin-tone shifts — whenever 2+ people are involved),
not several unrelated bugs. Worth validating directly with eng/product whether
the "couples edition" / "kiss feature" were deliberately pulled (moderation risk,
per the GB review about explicit-content false positives) or lost in a refactor.

## Non-English findings not yet formalized into a category (found by manual read)

These matched a request-signal in their language but didn't fit an existing
keyword category — read by hand rather than regex-classified. Real, specific
asks worth tracking even at low volume:

- **Object removal tool was removed** *(ES/MX)*: "there used to be an option to
  erase objects with AI and they disabled it"
- **Explicit batch request** *(KO/KR)*: "it would be nice to have a feature to
  select multiple photos and process them all at once"
- **AI hallucinating faces on non-portrait content** *(KO/KR)*: "I was shocked
  to see it somehow creates a face and creates eyes in a strange place" —
  distinct from the group-photo hallucination, this is on photos that shouldn't
  have a face detected at all
- **Cross-platform subscription bundling** *(KO/KR)*: "I also use the desktop
  web Remini — it would be nice to bundle both into one subscription"
- **Student pricing tier** *(PT/BR)*: "could create a more affordable student
  plan"
- **Toggle to fully disable AI/auto-enhancement** *(PT/BR, KO/KR)*: multiple
  independent asks for a way to turn off automatic face retouching entirely,
  not just adjust its intensity
- **Watermark persists after paying** *(PT/BR)*: "if I pay for premium I don't
  think there should be a watermark"
- **Hands/anatomy accuracy** *(ES/MX)*: "should improve the AI, DON'T MODIFY
  THE HANDS" — same theme as the English "extra arm/leg" finding

## Regional patterns worth flagging (not individual feature requests, but signal)

- **Turkey: a large, concentrated wave of "bring back the old version" complaints**
  (~30+ reviews, dwarfing the equivalent complaint volume in any other single
  market) about a recent update making results look "yapay" (artificial/fake),
  over-applying makeup/effects, and changing faces. Worth checking whether a
  Turkey-specific rollout, A/B test, or regional model version is the cause.
- **Indonesia: a concentrated pattern of unauthorized-charge/refund-denial
  complaints** — multiple independent users reporting being charged after a
  free trial with refund requests refused. Worth a billing/dark-pattern audit
  specific to the Indonesian storefront/payment flow.

## Open issues / next steps

1. **Play Store is still throttled.** App Store fetch is reliable at ~500/country;
   Play Store only succeeds for a handful of the 20 countries per run despite
   serialization, staggering, and retry/backoff — likely IP-level blocking on
   the fetch environment, not something fixable in code alone.
2. **App Store fetch already sorts by most-recent** (`sortby=mostrecent` in the
   RSS URL) — confirmed against the data (e.g., German App Store pull spans
   2024-07 to 2026-09, consistent with a genuinely time-ordered recent window).
   Apparent "randomness" between runs is Apple's feed being dynamic over time
   (new reviews push the oldest ones out of the top-500 window), not unsorted
   sampling on our end.
3. **More countries = more App Store volume**, roughly linearly (~500/country).
   We use 20 of ~175 storefronts. Good next candidates for the couples/
   multi-person segment specifically: more Spanish-speaking markets (AR, CO,
   CL, PE), Portugal (we only have Brazil), Arabic-speaking markets, core
   Russia.
4. **The long tail of ~60 minor languages (~556 reviews)** hasn't been
   individually screened — low expected value per language, but available on
   request.
